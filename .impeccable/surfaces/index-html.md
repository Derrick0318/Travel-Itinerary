Scope: index.html (the whole single-page trip companion). Mode: Operate (travellers check today's plan, weather, map and contacts on a phone during the tour).

Audience and job: everyone on the Brighton "一半烟火，一半未来" tour, 24–30 Nov 2026, opening one shared link; find today, the next stop, meals, tonight's hotel, the weather, and who to call. Constraints: always bilingual, adjustable text size, offline, nothing blocked in mainland China, never looks official.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The tour as a love letter sent home: postcards, airmail and a wax seal, after the film 《给阿嬷的情书》. Pinned by the user ("那天那个信封比较好看有趣"): the envelope world beat the retro-film redesign, which is kept on the retro-film branch.

OWN-WORLD: Cream paper by day, ink-night by dark; Chaozhou vermilion, ink indigo, gold foil. Airmail stripes on cards and passes, a red wax seal 信, a postmark with the departure date, gold-foil postage stamps for days. Titles in Noto Serif SC Bold, English subtitles in italic serif; body in the system sans. Phosphor icons, no emoji.

STORY: A traveller sees the countdown (or Day N) on a postmarked card, checks the boarding passes, flips through day postcards via stamp chips, then weather, map and contacts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed Guangji Bridge photo, gold letter-by-letter title 一半烟火，一半未来, vertical 给阿嬷的情书, wax seal; the countdown card with postmark overlaps the photo's bottom edge.

FORM: Operate shell (top bar, floating bottom tabs, one scroll). Signature move: day postcards with gold-foil stamps that stamp in and shimmer once. User-pinned direction, no concept-seed roll. Code-led (no image generation).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
