## Direction contract

THESIS: The tour told as the film it follows: seven scenes with title cards and subtitles. Refuses the cream love-letter travel journal and the sky-blue booking site.

OWN-WORLD: Silver-screen grey ground, projector-black film and ink, one committed film-stock yellow carrying the header bar and the active frame, cinnabar red only for "today" and live state. Titles in Noto Serif SC Bold like Chinese film title cards; body in the system sans. Every string set as a subtitle pair: Chinese line, English line beneath in a lighter, smaller weight. Frame-like 6px corners; perforations only on the film strip.

STORY: On the coach a traveller sees the countdown or "Day N", today's stops, meals and hotel; flicks the strip to any day; checks weather and the map; taps to call the guide.
